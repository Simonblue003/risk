RISKPILOT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [25/4/2026]

RiskPilot helps users plan trades, record journal entries, track rules, and review performance. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide

  • Google account information used to sign in, such as your email address.
  • Trade entries you create, including instruments, entry price, stop loss, take profit, risk, and outcomes.
  • Journal notes, discipline notes, rule tracking, and reflections you choose to save.

b. Information stored locally

  • If you use guest mode, your app data may be stored locally on your device.

c. Information stored in the cloud

  • If you sign in, your account data and app content may be stored in our backend to sync your experience across devices.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide calculator, journal, discipline, and statistics features.
  • To save and sync user data when signed in.
  • To improve app functionality, reliability, and user experience.
  • To respond to support and account requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, and storage providers.

4. Guest Mode

If you use RiskPilot as a guest, some or all of your data may remain only on your device and may not be recoverable if the app is removed or the device is reset.

5. Account Deletion

If you created an account and want it deleted, please visit our account deletion page: Delete Account.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

RiskPilot is not intended for children under 13, or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

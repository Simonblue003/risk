Account Deletion

If you created a RiskPilot account and want your account deleted, please send a request using the contact method below.

How to request deletion

Email us at: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]

Please include:

  • The Google email address linked to your RiskPilot account
  • The subject line: RiskPilot Account Deletion Request

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile information
  • Your synced trades
  • Your synced settings and discipline-related synced records

What may be retained

  • Records we must keep temporarily for legal, fraud-prevention, tax, security, or compliance reasons
  • Anonymous or aggregated analytics that no longer identify you

Guest mode data

If you used RiskPilot in guest mode, data stored only on your device may need to be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s local storage.
